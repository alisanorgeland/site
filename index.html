About me

Hi, I'm Alisa

HR specialist and career consultant in Norway. I help people from all over the world understand how hiring here really works

My path

Before Norway

I was a lawyer at a large international pharmaceutical company. Long days, a lot of responsibility and two small kids at home

Starting again

Ten years ago we moved to Norway with our family. New language, new system, new rules. I knew one thing: I had to learn Norwegian - and I reached B2 in 13 months

Inside the system

I worked as a career advisor at NAV and as an HR specialist. I've run more than 370 interviews and seen the job market from both sides of the table

Today

I help people find their way into the Norwegian job market - calmly, honestly and step by step. Through my blog, my YouTube channel, the CV Guide and 1:1 sessions

Life outside work

Two hockey-playing sons, long road trips to tournaments, runs in the forest, autumn hikes in the mountains and our dog Loke, who's always by my side
